# Unfolda Logo — Final Version

Based on Nano Banana generation, simplified to level 2 (3 text lines).

## Concept

Open book with filled pages. Left page (navy #1a1f36) = original text. Right page (amber #c9963a) = translation. Three text lines on each page suggest readable content. Dark spine separates the two languages.

The slight curve on the left page's outer edge gives the book dimensionality without being illustrative.

## Minimum sizes

- Favicon: 16px (lines omitted at this size — use pure shape)

## Figma finishing checklist

1. Replace Inter wordmark with Satoshi Medium
2. Adjust kerning — especially the "nf" and "da" pairs
3. Align baseline of wordmark with ~65% height of icon
4. Export: SVG, PNG @1x @2x @3x, ICO for favicon
5. Test on: white, cream, navy, and image backgrounds
