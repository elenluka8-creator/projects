Original

Era una bella mattina di fine novembre. Nella notte aveva piovuto un poco...

Translation

It was a beautiful morning at the end of November. During the night it had rained a little, and the ground was damp, as if a watery breath were rising from the soil. But toward morning the sky had cleared.

Notes

"un respiro acquoso" — literally "a watery breath." A literary metaphor for ground moisture rising as mist.

"si era rasserenato" — from "rasserenare" (to clear up). Often used for both weather and mood.
